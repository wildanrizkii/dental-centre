## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-touch specialist aesthetics and bespoke restorative care as an intimate, cocoon-like nocturnal lounge. By inverting sterile healthcare conventions into a subterranean, dimly lit private suite, the experience prioritizes serene confidentiality, sensory comfort, and quiet luxury.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Nocturnal Intimacy:** Immersive charcoal foundations eliminate eye strain and replace clinical exposure with a sheltered, private sanctuary.
- **Sculpted Geometric Modernism:** Unifying the voice under a single modern geometric sans-serif typeface lends architectural clarity, contemporary poise, and an approachable yet disciplined edge.
- **Pebble & Capsule Contours:** Dramatic pill silhouettes and deeply curved surfaces provide smooth, tactile touchpoints reminiscent of hand-smoothed river stones and bespoke curved architectural joinery.

## Colors

The palette establishes a nocturnal sanctuary built upon deep graphite minerals, soft glowing taupe highlights, and calming botanical accents:

- **Primary (`#303030` - Deep Charcoal Base):** The foundational anchor for elevated structural elements, interactive controls, and tactile containers. It establishes depth without absolute black voidness.
- **Secondary (`#B7A89A` - Warm Taupe Luminescence):** The luminous voice of the interface. Employed as an organic beacon for focal buttons, prominent pill accents, and glowing interaction states against the nocturnal backdrop.
- **Tertiary (`#A5AA98` - Muted Sage):** A restorative botanical nuance that signals positive states, completed milestones, holistic health telemetry, and calming indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1B1C1C` - Nocturnal Ground):** The immersive canvas surface. Absorbs harsh reflections, creating a continuous dark room ambiance for high-focus consultation.
- **Taupe Glow (`#D4C4B5`):** A high-luminance stone tone applied to prominent headlines, primary iconography, and active toggle surfaces.
- **Hairline Stone (`#443E37`):** A warm, low-contrast rim tone for subtle container separation and borders.

## Typography

Typography is unified comprehensively under Plus Jakarta Sans, establishing modern architectural balance, clean legibility, and geometric warmth throughout every scale of the interface.

- **Headlines & Display:** Formed with tight letter tracking and purposeful weights (`500` and `600`). The geometric rhythm and generous lowercase counters create a forward-thinking, boutique presence while rejecting cold institutional formality.
- **Body & Functional Text:** Standardized at weight `400` with extended line heights (`1.6` to `1.75`) to guarantee fatigue-free reading on dark backdrops. Deep charcoal surfaces paired with off-white taupe text ensure high readability without optical glare.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with semibold and bold weights along with deliberate micro-tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`), maintaining crisp definition on small pill tags, technical telemetry, and metadata badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface deploys an architectural fluid grid designed for spacious pacing, rhythmic grouping, and lounge-like contemplation:

- **Grid Mechanics:** A fluid 12-column system governs desktop screens, collapsing cleanly to 8 columns on tablets and 4 columns on mobile viewports.
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop compositions utilize expansive `4rem` margins and `2.5rem` gutters to echo high-end editorial layouts. Mobile environments shift to `1.25rem` outer canvas padding with `1.5rem` column gutters for comfortable thumb reach.
- **Component Intervals:** Dense atomic relationships (within inputs, badges, and avatars) employ `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Structural cards and section stacks employ `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to ensure quiet pauses between functional blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

In this nocturnal environment, elevation avoids harsh drop shadows in favor of tonal layering, warm interior glows, and delicate low-contrast outlines:

- **Tonal Stepping:** The canvas rests on `#1B1C1C`. Elevated cards and modules step upward into `#222323` and `#303030`, simulating physical planes cut from dark, smooth basalt.
- **Warm Ambient Bloom:** Floating menus, active sheets, and high-priority modals utilize an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow combined with a subtle inner rim light:
  - Resting Card: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4), inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(212, 196, 181, 0.08)`
  - Elevated Container / Hover: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.12), inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(212, 196, 181, 0.15)`
  - Modal Backdrop: `0 28px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.08)`
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Panels are delineated using 1px hairline strokes in `#443E37` or `rgba(212, 196, 181, 0.12)`, framing each module cleanly without breaking the nocturnal mood.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an organic, pill-centric geometry (level `3`) that softens the architectural darkness into an inviting, approachable sanctuary:

- **Pill Silhouettes (`9999px`):** Used universally across primary and secondary buttons, status badges, segment toggles, and filter chips. The complete capsule geometry reinforces a tactile, pebble-like quality.
- **Substantial Card Curvature (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / `2rem` - `3rem`):** Media canvases, diagnostic summaries, and client record cards employ sweeping rounded boundaries that mimic smooth river stone basins.
- **Input Fields & Form Enclosures:** Bound with `1rem` (`rounded-xl`) outer perimeters to provide distinct container clarity while sustaining the soft aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** High-contrast Warm Taupe (`#B7A89A`) fill with deep charcoal text (`#1B1C1C`), set in `label-lg` with `0.875rem 2rem` padding. On hover, blooms with a soft taupe outer halo (`0 0 16px rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.3)`) and transitions to `#D4C4B5`.
- **Secondary Nocturnal Pill:** Charcoal container (`#303030`) with Warm Taupe text (`#D4C4B5`) and a 1px border of `rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.25)`. Transitions to `rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.15)` fill on hover.
- **Ghost Pill:** Transparent fill, Warm Taupe text, 1px hairline border in `rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.2)`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Capsule Chips:** Fully pill-shaped with `0.375rem 1rem` padding. Default states feature a `#222323` fill with a subtle stone rim. Selected states transition to Muted Sage (`#A5AA98`) with charcoal text (`#1B1C1C`) or glowing taupe with charcoal text.

### Form Inputs
- **Fields:** Grounded in `#222323` with a 1px border of `rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.2)`, `1rem` corner rounding, and `0.875rem 1.25rem` internal padding. Text is set in `#D4C4B5`.
- **Focus State:** Transitions the border to luminous taupe (`#D4C4B5`) with an ambient outer glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.2)`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 6px rounded-square enclosures with `#222323` base and a 1px stone border. When checked, animates to a solid `#B7A89A` fill with a `#1B1C1C` check glyph.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular capsule nodes. Selected state displays a glowing taupe center dot nested inside a `#303030` crater.

### Cards & Sanctuary Modules
- **Standard Card:** Layered on `#222323` with a `2rem` border radius, hairline stone border (`1px solid rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.12)`), and `space-xl` padding.
- **Spotlight Capsule Panel:** Set in `#303030` with `3rem` rounded perimeters and ambient inset rim lighting, highlighting treatment previews and case studies.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Clinical Telemetry & Progress Node:** A vertical rail rendered in `#443E37` with glowing pill node badges indicating procedural phases, healing timelines, and recovery milestones.
- **Practitioner Portfolio Module:** An asymmetric card pairing a 3rem rounded portrait tile with integrated credential tags in Plus Jakarta Sans semibold and an instant consultation trigger pill.
