Phase 01 • Diagnostic Day 1

Multi-Spectral Volumetric Mapping

Ultra-low radiation CBCT paired with dynamic jaw tracking and polarization intraoral photography to calculate bone density, nerve canals, and micro-wear.

Phase 02 • Pre-Surgical Day 3

Cad-Cam Biomimetic Mock-Up

Before clinical intervention, patients test a functional prototype directly in their mouth. Phonetics, occlusion, and lip dynamics are perfected with Zero-Tooth-Reduction mockups.

Phase 03 • Intervention Day 7

Minimally Invasive Operating Suite

Guided surgical navigation systems ensure computer-placed implants with micronic margin tolerances under intravenous twilight sedation for absolute physiological comfort.

Phase 04 • Sanctuary Care Day 7+

Circadian Post-Operative Lounge

Post-treatment hyperbaric oxygen recovery protocols, tailored anti-inflammatory infusions, and dedicated private chauffeur transit back to residence.
