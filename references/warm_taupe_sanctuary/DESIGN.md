## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a tranquil, architectural sanctuary for high-end specialist dental care and oral aesthetics. Drawing from modern wellness retreats, boutique hospitality, and luxury editorial design, it shifts the healthcare narrative from clinical coldness to restorative comfort. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Warm Minimalist Architecture:** Generous negative space, soft ambient light, and unhurried layouts that reduce patient anxiety.
- **Editorial Literary Sophistication:** The deliberate contrast between classical, intellectual serif typography and welcoming, geometric sans-serif details creates a bespoke, high-touch aura.
- **Organic Fluidity:** Pill-shaped interactions, deep-radii cards, and whisper-quiet outlines evoke natural pebbles, stone surfaces, and bespoke architectural curves.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in mineral earth tones, warm limestone, and serene botanical nuances:

- **Primary (`#B7A89A` - Warm Taupe):** The foundational anchor. Conveys tactile stone, linen textures, and understated warmth. Used for primary brand moments, key actions, and highlighted structural accents.
- **Secondary (`#A5AA98` - Muted Sage):** A calming botanical green with grey undertones. Represents precision, cellular restoration, and tranquility. Applied to secondary controls, reassuring confirmations, badges, and wellness callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#EDE2D8` - Soft Beige):** A delicate, warm surface tone reminiscent of unbleached silk and travertine marble. Acts as secondary container fills and tonal grouping elements.
- **Neutral (`#292929` - Deep Charcoal):** Replaces harsh jet black with an organic, ink-like deep charcoal for text and high-contrast structural iconography.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8F6F2` - Warm Ivory):** The warm, non-glare background canvas that prevents clinical eye strain while exuding relaxed warmth.
- **Surface Highlight (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for active interactive cards, modal backdrops, and elevated content panels to achieve layered depth without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a pairing of literary tradition and modern functional clarity:

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Set with deliberate tracking and natural leading. Gives clinical specialisms (prosthodontics, periodontics, cosmetic dentistry) the prestige of a bespoke atelier. Large display titles should emphasize natural casing rather than uppercase locks.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, welcoming, and legible at small scales. Generous counters and soft curves complement the warmth of the palette while delivering precision for appointment slots, treatment plans, and medical charts.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small-caps treatments should leverage slight letter-spacing (`0.03em` - `0.06em`) to preserve legibility across tags and category chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop experiences utilize spacious 4rem outer margins and 2.5rem gutters to mirror editorial magazine spreads. Mobile views compress gracefully to 1.25rem margins and 1.5rem gutters.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous breathing room between sections (`space-2xl` / 4rem) reinforces an unhurried clinical environment. Groupings within cards maintain tighter relationships (`space-sm` to `space-md`).
- **Asymmetry & Alignment:** Hero areas and specialist profiles should interleave imagery and textual content with asymmetrical 7/5 or 8/4 column balances rather than rigid split halves.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with subtle realism inspired by natural daylight diffusing across plaster walls. Heavy drop shadows and glossy neon surfaces are prohibited.

- **Surface Tiers:** Content elevation is achieved through color shift rather than sharp borders: the canvas sits on Warm Ivory (`#F8F6F2`), grouping containers rest on Soft Beige (`#EDE2D8`), and actionable floating cards lift onto Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Warm Ambient Diffusion:** When shadows are required for floating drawers, modals, or hover states, they must use an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted formula:
  - Card resting: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(41, 41, 41, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.08)`
  - Elevated/Hover: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(41, 41, 41, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.12)`
  - Overlays/Modals: `0 32px 64px -12px rgba(41, 41, 41, 0.14)`
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Subtle borders use 1px solid taupe tints (`rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.25)`) to delineate sections without introducing harsh geometric dividing lines.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, pebble-inspired geometry with a primary roundedness scale of `3` (pill and deep radii):

- **Pill-shaped Elements (Full Radius / `rounded-full`):** Buttons, interactive filter chips, badges, and status pills are fully rounded to feel tactile and pleasant to touch.
- **Large Container Radii (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 2rem - 3rem):** Feature cards, medical record dashboards, and imagery containers employ 32px to 48px radii, mirroring contemporary architectural coved ceilings and rounded curved walls.
- **Input Fields & Small Cards:** Follow a 1rem (`rounded-xl`) radius to retain clear form affordance while honoring the soft aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Warm Taupe background (`#B7A89A`), Pure White text (`#FFFFFF`), fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to Deep Charcoal (`#292929`).
- **Secondary:** Soft Beige fill (`#EDE2D8`), Deep Charcoal text (`#292929`), subtle hover transition to Muted Sage (`#A5AA98`) with white text.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, Deep Charcoal text, 1px hairline border in `rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.4)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Specialism Chips:** Fully pill-shaped with `0.375rem 1rem` padding. Inactive chips use Soft Beige background with charcoal text; selected chips use Muted Sage (`#A5AA98`) with white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Pure White background with a 1px border of `rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.4)`, 1rem (`rounded-xl`) corners. Labels sit outside in `label-sm` or `label-md` Deep Charcoal.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border colored in Warm Taupe (`#B7A89A`) accompanied by an ambient halo `0 0 0 4px rgba(183, 168, 154, 0.15)`. No default blue browser focus rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and 6px rounded-square checkboxes. Checked fill transitions to Deep Charcoal or Muted Sage with a crisp Pure White check icon.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Card:** Pure White background, 2rem (`rounded-2xl`) border radius, warm ambient drop shadow, and `space-xl` internal padding.
- **Spotlight / Feature Panel:** Soft Beige background (`#EDE2D8`), 3rem (`rounded-3xl`) corners, zero shadow, relying entirely on clean typography and generous whitespace.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Treatment Timeline / Plan:** Clean vertical track rendered in Muted Sage hairline with pill-shaped node badges, displaying stage milestones and recovery indicators.
- **Practitioner Bio Tile:** Asymmetric portrait container with an arched or 3rem top-left radius, integrated rating, credentials in Garamond italic, and consultation trigger pill button.
